# CNC_MP
📘 CNC Computer Science Exams and Corrections

This repository contains a collection of past CNC (Concours National Commun) Computer Science exams along with their detailed solutions and corrections. It is intended to support students preparing for the CNC competitive exams, offering a valuable resource for practice and self-assessment.

Contents:
📝 CNC Computer Science exam papers (by year)

✅ Detailed corrections and solutions

📂 Organized by year and subject

Purpose:
To help students prepare effectively for CNC Computer Science exams by providing access to past papers and clear, well-explained corrections.
